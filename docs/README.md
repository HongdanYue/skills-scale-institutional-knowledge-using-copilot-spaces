# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management Docs. This README provides an overview of our project management processes and serves as a central navigation hub for all team members learning about how OctoAcme runs projects.

## OctoAcme Approach

OctoAcme runs projects with a focus on five core principles:

- **Customer-first delivery**: Prioritize customer value and usability in all decisions
- **Iterative execution**: Deliver small, testable increments rather than large batches
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has named Project Managers and Product Leads who own outcomes
- **Data-driven decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence, not assumptions
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback, learning, and blameless problem-solving

## Core Roles

Every OctoAcme project operates with defined roles and responsibilities:

- **Project Manager (PM)**: Coordinates delivery activities, manages schedules, risks, and communications
- **Product Manager (PdM)**: Defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, and measures success
- **Developers**: Implement features, collaborate on design, and ensure testability
- **QA/Testing**: Validate quality and confirm acceptance criteria are met
- **Stakeholders**: Provide inputs, feedback, and approvals

For detailed persona descriptions and responsibilities, see [OctoAcme Personas](./octoacme-roles-and-personas.md).

## Project Lifecycle

OctoAcme projects follow a structured five-phase lifecycle:

1. **Initiation**: Define the problem statement, identify stakeholders, and establish a high-level timeline
2. **Planning**: Break work into actionable increments, define scope, allocate resources, and map milestones
3. **Execution**: Build, test, review, and iterate based on feedback and quality standards
4. **Release**: Deploy to production, verify functionality, and communicate with stakeholders
5. **Close & Retrospective**: Capture learnings and identify improvements for future projects

## Communication Cadence

- **Weekly sync** between PM and Product Manager
- **Twice-weekly standups** for delivery team (or as agreed)
- **Monthly stakeholder updates** on progress and risks
- **Ad-hoc escalations** for critical blockers or decisions

## Key Artifacts

Throughout the project lifecycle, your team will create and maintain:

- **Project Charter / One-pager** — Problem statement, goals, success metrics, and timeline
- **Backlog** — Prioritized list of work items with acceptance criteria
- **Release Plan** — Milestones, dependencies, and target dates
- **Risk Register** — Tracked risks with owners, impact, and mitigation plans
- **Project Board** — Visual workflow of work (Backlog → Ready → In Progress → In Review → QA → Done)
- **Retrospective Notes** — Learnings and action items after sprints or milestones
