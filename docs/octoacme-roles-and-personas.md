# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads own the quality strategy and ensure all work meets acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They collaborate with product, development, and stakeholders to define testability requirements and validate delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with project requirements
- Define acceptance criteria alongside Product Managers and Developers
- Execute manual and automated testing activities
- Track defects, quality metrics, and test coverage
- Coordinate with security and performance testing when needed
- Validate that Definition of Done includes quality standards
- Review user stories for testability and completeness

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet user expectations
- Identify risks and issues early in the delivery cycle
- Maintain comprehensive test coverage and traceability
- Reduce escaped defects and post-release issues

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria reviews
- Daily standups to report testing progress and blockers
- Quality metrics dashboards and release readiness reports
- Post-release validation and feedback

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define technical direction, ensure design quality, and manage technical debt and architecture decisions. They work closely with developers to maintain system reliability, scalability, and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions that meet non-functional requirements (performance, scalability, security)
- Review code and architectural decisions for quality and consistency
- Identify and communicate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor developers and promote best practices
- Track and prioritize technical debt work
- Participate in capacity planning, estimation, and risk assessment
- Collaborate with DevOps on infrastructure and deployment considerations

### Goals
- Maintain system reliability, scalability, and maintainability
- Reduce cycle time through good design and automation
- Build team technical capability and knowledge sharing
- Balance feature velocity with long-term technical health

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review and feedback
- Technical risk escalations
- Mentoring and knowledge-sharing sessions
- Architecture decision records (ADRs) and design documentation

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach the team on continuous improvement. They help teams adopt and optimize agile practices while protecting team focus and velocity.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove blockers and impediments
- Coach team members on agile practices and mindset
- Maintain sprint artifacts and project visibility
- Promote psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Shield team from external distractions and scope creep
- Track velocity and help team identify process improvements

### Goals
- Enable teams to deliver consistently and predictably
- Foster a culture of continuous learning and improvement
- Maximize team productivity and morale
- Support organizational agile transformation

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies facilitation
- One-on-ones to understand and address blocker
- Retrospective action item tracking and follow-up
- Coaching and feedback on agile practices

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads define user experience requirements, ensure usability standards, and advocate for user needs throughout the delivery process. They collaborate with product and development to create intuitive, accessible solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define user personas and journey maps
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Define usability standards and accessibility requirements (e.g., WCAG compliance)
- Review user interface implementations for alignment with design standards
- Validate solutions with users through testing and feedback
- Collaborate with developers on technical feasibility and implementation details
- Participate in acceptance criteria definition and design reviews

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences that delight customers
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Maintain design consistency and brand standards
- Ensure solutions meet usability and accessibility requirements

### Typical Communication
- Design kickoffs and user research findings
- Design reviews and feedback on implementations
- Acceptance criteria and user story refinement
- Usability testing reports and user feedback synthesis

---

## DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Infrastructure Engineers manage deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and operational readiness. They enable reliable, secure, and scalable delivery of software to production.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment pipelines (CI/CD)
- Manage infrastructure, environments, and configuration
- Monitor system health, performance, and security
- Coordinate with QA and developers on testing environments
- Implement observability, logging, and alerting
- Document operational procedures and runbooks
- Participate in release planning and rollback procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and secure deployments
- Maintain system uptime and performance
- Reduce deployment risk and cycle time
- Support operational excellence and incident response

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- Infrastructure readiness reviews
- Performance and monitoring dashboards
- Incident response and post-mortem participation
- Operational documentation and training

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, priorities, and executive oversight. They ensure projects align with business goals and remove organizational blockers to enable team success.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals and success metrics for projects
- Provide budget and resource approval
- Escalate organizational blockers and dependencies
- Provide input on prioritization and trade-offs
- Review and approve major releases and milestones
- Communicate project status to broader organization
- Support team with cross-functional alignment and decisions

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and ROI
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Enable team success through stakeholder support
- Reduce business risk and organizational friction

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and status reports
- Approval gates and decision meetings
- Business case and success metrics reviews
- Executive briefings and communication

- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Cross-functional interactions between personas help illustrate dependencies and communication patterns in OctoAcme project delivery.
